# Marginal Probability

Marginalization is covered in [[Joint Distributions]].

→ [[Joint Distributions#Marginal Distributions]]

For discrete random variables:

$$
p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y)
$$

and:

$$
p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y).
$$

This note is an index entry pointing to the canonical explanation rather than duplicating it.
